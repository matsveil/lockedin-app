# Privacy Policy for LockedIn

**Last Updated: [Date]**

Thank you for choosing **LockedIn** (“we,” “us,” or “our”). We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how LockedIn collects, uses, and handles your information when you use our iOS application (“App”). By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

---

## 1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies solely to information collected by the LockedIn iOS App. It does not apply to any external websites, services, or third-party payment processors, except as outlined below (see [Section 4: Payment Processing](#4-payment-processing)). Please read this policy carefully to understand how we handle your information.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

1. **Personal Data Stored on Your Device**  
   - LockedIn may collect certain personal data (such as user preferences, settings, or App usage habits) exclusively to personalize and enhance your experience.  
   - All personal data collected by the App is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to our servers or shared with third parties (except for payment processing as described in [Section 4](#4-payment-processing)).

2. **Screen Time Data (Optional)**  
   - If you choose to grant LockedIn access to the Screen Time API on your device, we only use that data locally to display insights or provide features within the App. This data is never transmitted off the device.

3. **Health Data (Optional)**  
   - If you choose to grant LockedIn access to HealthKit, LockedIn may write Mindful Minutes to Apple Health. We do not read or collect any other health data, and we do not store or share any health data outside of what is necessary to write Mindful Minutes.

4. **No Device Information or Usage Data**  
   - We do not collect or analyze device information (e.g., device model, operating system version) or usage data (e.g., how often you open the App) beyond what is locally stored for your personal App experience.

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information

- **To Provide and Improve Our Service**  
  We use data stored on your device to enable App functionality, personalize content, and make LockedIn a better experience for you. All data remains on your device and is used solely for these purposes.

- **To Respect Your Choices**  
  The App only accesses Screen Time data and Health data (Mindful Minutes) if you grant explicit permission. If you do not grant permission, the App will continue to function without these features.

---

## 4. Payment Processing

- **RevenueCat**  
  LockedIn uses [RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/) as its third-party payment processor to handle subscriptions or one-time purchases. RevenueCat may collect and process payment-related information according to its own privacy policy. We recommend reviewing RevenueCat’s privacy policy for more details on their data handling practices.  
  - Any payment-related information (such as purchase receipts) may pass securely through RevenueCat’s servers to validate and manage your subscription.  
  - LockedIn does not receive or store your full payment details (e.g., credit card numbers) at any point.

---

## 5. Data Retention

- **On Your Device**  
  All personal data is stored on your device for as long as you use the App. If you delete the App, any data stored within the App on your device will also be deleted.

- **Payments**  
  Payment records are handled by RevenueCat according to their retention policy. Please refer to RevenueCat’s policy for more details.

---

## 6. Security

- **Local Storage**  
  We store and manage your data on your device in a manner intended to prevent unauthorized access or disclosure. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is fully secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of information stored locally.

- **Third-Party Links and Tools**  
  LockedIn may contain links to or integrations with third parties (e.g., RevenueCat). These third-party services have their own privacy policies and security practices. We encourage you to review those policies before disclosing any information.

LockedIn is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13 without parental consent. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us so we can promptly delete it.
